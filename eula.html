1. What the App does

The App lets authorized store personnel scan products, assemble an order in Provider's system, display order details on a selected Clover device, and request a card-present payment through Clover. The App is not a replacement for Clover's payment-processing terms or Merchant's agreement with Clover. Clover, rather than the App, handles card entry and payment processing on the device.
