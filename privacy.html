Korting Grocery Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Korting Outlet ("we," "us") provides Korting Grocery, a point-of-sale workflow for authorized store personnel. This policy describes information handled through that workflow and its connection to Clover. It does not replace the store's own customer privacy notice or Clover's privacy notice.

Information we handle

  • Store and user information: merchant and warehouse identifiers, employee or user identifiers, and the Clover device selected for a transaction.
  • Order information: scanned item codes, product descriptions, quantities, prices, sales-tax treatment, totals, order status, and transaction timestamps. Item codes are checked against Korting Outlet's internal product and inventory catalog, which it maintains for its stores; product descriptions come from that catalog when available.
  • Clover connection and payment information: authorization and refresh credentials needed to connect the merchant account, Clover device identifiers, payment and transaction identifiers, payment and tax amounts, and payment status. We do not store payment-card numbers or card security codes.
  • Operational information: technical logs and error details used to run, secure, and troubleshoot the service. Korting Grocery does not store IP addresses, browser identifiers, or cookies, and does not use analytics tracking.

Customers enter payment-card information on the Clover device, and Clover handles the payment-card details. The Korting Grocery checkout workflow does not ask customers to enter card numbers or card security codes into our website, and we do not store those details.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate the merchant connection, display an order on the selected Clover device, request and reconcile payment, maintain order records, calculate and report transaction totals, support store operations, investigate errors, and protect the service.

Who receives information

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising or marketing. To provide the checkout workflow, we send the device-display information and payment request data needed for a transaction to Clover. Our hosting, database, and support providers may process information on our behalf to operate the service. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the service.

Retention and security

We retain order and payment records for 10 years. We make daily database backups that are available for up to two weeks. We keep Clover authorization credentials while a merchant connection is active and delete or replace them when no longer needed, subject to the backup-retention period. We use access controls and other measures intended to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

Merchants can contact us about access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with their use of the app. Requests concerning a customer's payment-card data or Clover account data may also need to be directed to Clover or the merchant that processed the sale. We will respond as required by applicable law and our obligations to the merchant.

Changes

We may update this policy as the workflow or our data practices change. We will post the updated version here with a new effective date.
